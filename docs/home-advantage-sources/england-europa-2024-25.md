# English guaranteed Europa profiles, 2024/25

The `bournemouth-palace-2024-25.json` batch contains all 38 Premier League home matches for Bournemouth and Crystal Palace, 19 per club.

- Results source: OpenFootball England, complete 2024/25 Premier League season.
- Strength source: the project's generated 2026 UEFA club-coefficient snapshot.
- English opponents without a higher individual value use the `23.903` association floor.
- Historical pot fields remain neutral at `1`.
- Under the current coefficient snapshot, neither Bournemouth nor Crystal Palace has a weaker-opponent observation.

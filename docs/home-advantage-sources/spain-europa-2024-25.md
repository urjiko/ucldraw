# Spanish guaranteed Europa profiles, 2024/25

The `celta-sociedad-2024-25.json` batch contains all 38 La Liga home matches for Celta Vigo and Real Sociedad, 19 per club.

- Results source: OpenFootball Spain, complete 2024/25 Primera División season.
- Strength source: the project's generated 2026 UEFA club-coefficient snapshot.
- Spanish opponents without a higher individual value use the `19.409` association floor.
- Historical pot fields remain neutral at `1`.
- Under the current coefficient snapshot, neither Celta Vigo nor Real Sociedad has a weaker-opponent observation.
